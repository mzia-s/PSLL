PSLL: Persistent Sheaf Laplacian Learning for Protein-Ligand Binding Affinity Prediction
Software environment and reproducibility notes

Environment
  Python 3.11.3 (HPCC module Python/3.11.3-GCCcore-12.3.0)
  Operating system: Linux (SLURM cluster); feature generation and training on CPU

Packages (pip freeze of the feature-generation and training environment)
  gudhi          3.11.0    Vietoris-Rips and alpha complexes (simplex trees, filtration values)
  numpy          1.26.4    charge-weighted incidence matrices and eigenvalues (numpy.linalg.eigvalsh)
  scipy          1.17.1    Pearson correlation (scipy.stats.pearsonr)
  pandas         2.3.0     PQR / mol2 parsing, feature tables
  scikit-learn   1.7.0     GradientBoostingRegressor, StandardScaler
  fair-esm       2.0.0     ESM-2 protein embeddings
  torch          2.1.2+cpu
  pdb2pqr        3.7.1     protein partial charges (AMBER force field)

Ligand fingerprints
  Publicly released BT-FP code (bt_pro/generate_bt_fps.py) with the pretrained checkpoint
  chembl27_pubchem_zinc_512 (Chen, Zheng, Wei, Pan, J. Phys. Chem. Lett. 2021, 12, 10793-10801).
  The 512-dimensional descriptor is the final-layer hidden state of the first (<s>) token.

Protein embeddings
  Checkpoint esm2_t33_650M_UR50D, representation layer 33, mean over residue positions
  (beginning- and end-of-sequence tokens excluded), no fine-tuning, no truncation.
  Sequence: RCSB FASTA record of the entry; first protein chain for multi-chain entries.

Partial charges
  Protein: PDB2PQR 3.7.1, AMBER force field; hydrogens removed after charge assignment,
  no redistribution of hydrogen charges; HETATM records excluded.
  Ligand: Gasteiger-Huckel charges from the PDBbind mol2 files.

Random seeds
  0, 1, ..., 19 (random_state of GradientBoostingRegressor; one SLURM array task per seed).

Gradient boosting hyperparameters (Table 4 of the paper)
  ES-PSLL      n_estimators 25000, learning_rate 0.001, subsample 0.3
  CS-PSLL      n_estimators 20000, learning_rate 0.002, subsample 0.8
  t33+SMILES   n_estimators 30000, learning_rate 0.002, subsample 0.8
  All models:  max_depth 7, min_samples_split 5, loss squared_error, max_features sqrt
  Consensus:   PSLL(ES, CS) = 0.5 ES + 0.5 CS; final = 0.3 ES + 0.3 CS + 0.4 t33+SMILES

Scripts
  readin.py        PQR and mol2 parsing (coordinates, elements, charges, residue names)
  PSL.py           PSLL features: cross-interface VR dimension-0 and alpha dimension-1
                   operators, 12 filtration values (1-12 A), 9 spectral statistics per block
  run_PSL.sh       SLURM array job for feature generation (ES cutoff 12 A, CS cutoff 10 A)
  embed_pdb.py, embed_pdb.sh    ESM-2 embeddings
  run_smiles.sh    BT-FP ligand fingerprints
  run_gbrt.py, gbrt_array.sh    training on the refined set, scoring on the core set, 20 seeds,
                   aggregation of scores and seed-averaged predictions
  PSL_v2007.ipynb  consensus of the three models and final metrics
  vXXXX_refine_list.csv, vXXXX_core_list.csv   training / test partitions (PDB codes and pK labels)

Data
  PDBbind v2007, v2013 and v2016 refined and core sets, https://www.pdbbind-plus.org.cn/
